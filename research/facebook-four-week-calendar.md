## Strategy: useful answers, not ads

Each post answers one question families actually ask, with the evidence that they ask it, and points to one live, sourced page. Topics, in order of evidence:

1. **"Does Medicare pay for assisted living?"** The site's most-searched question: 579 impressions across 32 query forms in the Make Search Console read of 25 Nov 2026. Page: /guides/does-medicare-pay-for-senior-care/ (live).
2. **"We noticed changes in Mom over the holidays. What now?"** Seasonal; the visits of 26 Nov to 1 Jan. Page: /guides/noticed-changes-in-parent-holiday-visit/. It is in package #91, so it is used only once live.
3. **"Illinois Medicaid will backdate only two months from 1 January."** HFS federal-changes FAQ, read 28 Nov 2026. Page: /guides/illinois-medicaid-pending-nursing-home/. Used only once the package carrying its corrected text (4815ca1) is live, and only before 1 January.
4. **Checklist (A) and assessment (C) hooks** as fallbacks where a topic page is not live yet.

The short answer is no: Medicare does not pay for assisted living, and it does not pay for long-term custodial nursing home care. I wrote a free, plain-language guide to what is and is not covered, and who does pay for assisted living in Illinois.
