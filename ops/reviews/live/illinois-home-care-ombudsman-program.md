## Required corrections

- C1 (scope). Source: "Are enrolled in the Dual Eligible Special Needs Plans (D-SNPs) and receive services from a managed care organization (MCO)". The short answer's "an Illinois Medicaid managed care plan (a D-SNP)" lets a parent in a Medicaid managed care plan that is not a D-SNP (for example a HealthChoice Illinois plan) think the programme covers them. Corrected: "If your parent is enrolled in a Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (D-SNP) and gets services from its managed care organisation, or gets home care through one of four Home and Community Based Services waivers ..."
