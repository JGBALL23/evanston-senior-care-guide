Required corrections
- C1. Short answer: "while a spouse, a child under 21, or a disabled child survives" becomes "while a spouse, a child under 21, or a blind or disabled child of any age survives". Source (Guide, Are there times when the state will not ask for money back?): "There is a child of any age who is blind or permanently and totally disabled under Social Security requirements."
- C2. Liens paragraph: "medical-assistance-only recipients in a long-term care facility ... but only after 120 consecutive days in the facility. No lien is filed if the home is occupied by a spouse, a child under 21, a disabled child of any age" becomes "people who receive AABD medical assistance only and live in a long-term care facility ... but only after 120 consecutive calendar days in a medical institution. No lien is filed if the home is occupied by a spouse, a child under 21, a child over 21 who is blind or disabled". Source (Liens and Estate Recovery): "If you receive AABD medical assistance only, reside in a long-term care facility, and have resided for at least 120 consecutive calendar days in a medical institution, a lien will be filed in the amount of all medical assistance received, both before and during the time in a long- term care facility." and "if your property is occupied by your spouse, your child under age 21, your child over age 21 who is blind or disabled or, in some cases your siblings."
