Required corrections (applied in accuracy PR #89):
1. Remove "only" (short answer, heading, discharge paragraph) and say the brochure is a summary that does not say these are the only reasons the law allows; ask the ombudsman whether a notice's reason is one the law allows. Source: "The facility can force you to leave because: ...you have not paid your bill, ...you are dangerous to yourself or others, ...your medical needs cannot be met, or ...the facility closes." and "For a full list of rights, talk to your ombudsman."
2. Short answer: the brochure speaks of long-term care facilities and does not say which of these rights apply in assisted living. Source: title "Residents' Rights for People in Long-Term Care Facilities"; no mention of assisted living in the brochure.
