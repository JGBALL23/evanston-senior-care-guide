Required corrections
- C1. Replace the could-not-find sentence with the Engage page's mandated-reporter rule (professionals delivering services to adults 60+ or people with disabilities 18-59 unable, due to dysfunction, to report for themselves, in social services, adult care, law enforcement, education, medicine, state service to seniors, social work) and its "Report it, even if you are not sure." Source (Engage, Who are Adult Protective Service mandated reporters? / What if I am not sure if I should make a report?).
- C2. Answer box and first section: the facility exclusion applies to problems with a facility's care; add the brochure's exception, quoting "the APS Program may respond." Source as in the table.
