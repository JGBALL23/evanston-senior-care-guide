## Required corrections

C-1 (C4, section "Who licenses memory care in Illinois assisted living", second paragraph). Replace "and adds why Medicare plays no part:" with "and adds why there is no federal oversight:".
Source sentence (Plan, "Assisted Living Facilities"): "This is a state licensure program with no federal oversight, as the residents in both assisted living and memory care areas are private pay through an establishment contract." The source is about oversight of the licensure program only; it does not say Medicare has no role in a resident's own medical care.

C-2 (Q2, answer box). Replace "staff serving people with dementia in an assisted living facility must take" with "staff serving people with dementia in an assisted living facility providing Alzheimer's or dementia services must take".
Source sentence (Plan, "Dementia-Specific Training Requirements"): "Staff serving clients with ADRD within a skilled nursing facility, assisted living facility, home health provider, or hospice program providing Alzheimer's or dementia services are required to take six hours of initial dementia training and three hours of annual dementia training thereafter."

C-3 (Q6, section "The training staff must have", first paragraph). Replace the sentence with: "According to IDPH's plan, staff serving people with Alzheimer's disease or a related dementia within a skilled nursing facility, assisted living facility, home health provider or hospice program providing Alzheimer's or dementia services are required to take "six hours of initial dementia training and three hours of annual dementia training thereafter."" (Keep the existing quotation characters.)
Source sentence: as for C-2.

C-4 (C10, section "The program disclosure"). Replace "A community that markets memory care has, in other words, described its program to the state in writing." with "In other words, a licensed community that provides specialized dementia care is required to have disclosed information about its program to IDPH, the agency that licenses assisted living."
Source sentences: Plan, "Quality Care Measures": "Illinois has an Alzheimer's Disease and Related Dementias Special Care Disclosure Act that requires licensed residential care settings that provide specialized care to individuals with dementia to disclose information about their program to the state agency responsible for licensing of that setting, as outlined in the act." IDPH AL page, "Assisted Living": "The Division of Assisted Living is responsible for all state licensure and survey processes for Assisted Living and Shared Housing establishments."

C-5 (C12, section "If private pay is not possible, and when to plan", first paragraph). Replace "families who cannot pay privately usually look at Illinois' Supportive Living Program." with "families who cannot pay privately may look at Illinois' Supportive Living Program, a Medicaid waiver program run by the Department of Healthcare and Family Services that IDPH's plan describes as an alternative to nursing home care "for low-income older people and people with physical disabilities ages 22-64"."
Source sentences: Plan, "Illinois Department of Healthcare and Family Services" (p. 27): "HFS oversees the Supportive Living Program (SLP), an approved HCBS waiver for assisted living services. Illinois developed SLP as an alternative to nursing home care for low-income older people and people with physical disabilities ages 22-64." (The quoted words match the source characters exactly.)
