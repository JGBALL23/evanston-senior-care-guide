Who can help my parent choose a Medicare plan for free in Illinois?

Short answer

Call the Illinois Senior Health Insurance Program (SHIP) on 1-800-252-8966 (711 TRS). It is a free, statewide counselling service for Medicare beneficiaries and their caregivers, run by the Illinois Department on Aging. SHIP is not affiliated with any insurance company, and its counsellors do not sell or solicit insurance of any kind — the advice is not trying to sell your parent anything.

A free, unbiased place to start

The Illinois Department on Aging describes the Senior Health Insurance Program, SHIP, as “a free statewide health insurance counseling service for Medicare beneficiaries and their caregivers.” It moved to the Department on Aging from the Illinois Department of Insurance in April 2013, and the programme itself dates back to 1988.

The Department states plainly that “SHIP is not affiliated with any insurance company” and that “SHIP counselors do not sell or solicit any type of insurance.” Medicare's own site makes the same point about the national SHIP network: “SHIPs aren't connected to any insurance companies or health plans.” That independence is the whole reason to call a SHIP counsellor before calling a plan's own sales line.

Picking the right Medicare plan is a different question from knowing whether Medicare will ever pay for the kind of care your parent eventually needs.

What a SHIP counsellor actually does

The Department's SHIP pages describe volunteer counsellors as the programme's “backbone,” trained and supported by the Department, with local sponsoring organisations providing office space, and reached “through the Area Agency on Aging network of offices throughout the state.” What a counsellor can help with, in the Department's own words, includes: educating consumers and answering questions about Medicare, Medicare Supplement insurance, long-term care insurance, Medicare HMOs and other health insurance; analysing Medicare Supplement and long-term care policies; organising and assisting with Medicare and Medicare Supplement claims; and one-on-one confidential counselling, with advocacy and referrals where appropriate. The Department also publishes an annual Medicare Choices Guide.

The SHIP page also lists related Department resources a counsellor can point a caller toward, without stating figures here: a Medicare Savings Programs chart, an Extra Help chart, and information on Medicaid income and asset limits. Ask the counsellor directly whether your parent's situation fits any of those — this guide is not going to estimate an income limit on the Department's behalf.

Medicare's own comparison tool, and where SHIP fits around it

Medicare.gov's own “Helpful tools” page points to its plan-comparison tool at medicare.gov/plan-compare, where you can “find and compare Medicare Advantage Plans (Part C),” other Medicare health plans and Medicare drug plans, review costs and services, and enter a parent's actual prescriptions to estimate what each plan would cost. A separate tool at medicare.gov/medigap-supplemental-insurance-plans finds Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policies and premiums by ZIP code.

The same Medicare.gov page names SHIP directly as the free, no-strings-attached help for using those tools: “Get free personalized health insurance counseling. SHIPs aren't connected to any insurance companies or health plans,” and says SHIP counsellors can help with comparing and joining plans. In other words, the self-serve comparison lives on Medicare.gov; the person who will sit with your parent and go through it is SHIP.

Medicare.gov also lists 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227, TTY 1-877-486-2048) as a federal line available around the clock except some holidays, and a live-chat option for finding local help. Neither replaces SHIP — they are simply other doors into the same federal programme.

What this guide could not pin down

Neither Department on Aging SHIP page this guide read states the counselling programme's hours, a typical wait for an appointment, or a list of named local counsellors by address. The Department instead points callers to a “Volunteers in Your Area by County” listing and a service locator rather than publishing that detail on the page itself.

This guide also will not quote a dollar figure for the Medicare Savings Programs or Extra Help, since doing so would mean reading it off a chart this guide did not open. Ask a SHIP counsellor directly whether either applies to your parent's income.

How to reach a counsellor

Call the Senior HelpLine on 1-800-252-8966 (711 TRS for deaf and speech-impaired callers), or email Aging.SHIP@illinois.gov. That is the same statewide number the Department publishes for its other aging programmes, so one call can also raise anything else covered on this site, from home care to caregiver support.

Where this fits with the rest of this site

A SHIP counsellor can help your parent pick the right Medicare plan. It cannot tell you whether Medicare will pay for the care your parent eventually needs — for that distinction, see Does Medicare pay for assisted living or nursing home care?
