Care Guides

How these guides are sourced and checked

Where the facts come from

Every figure, deadline and eligibility rule in a guide comes from a page that was opened and read, never from a search result or from memory. The sources are the bodies that run the programme or set the rule: Medicare.gov and the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Illinois state agencies, the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, the Illinois statutes, the City of Evanston and the Housing Authority of Cook County, plus the CareScout Cost of Care Survey for typical prices. A check in the site’s build refuses any other website in a guide’s list of sources.

One exception, and only in the cost guide: a community’s own website is used for that community’s own advertised prices, and the guide says so where the figures appear.

When a figure cannot be confirmed

If a figure cannot be read on one of those pages, it is left out. The guide says who sets it and whom to ask instead. A published maximum or standard is not presented as the amount a family will receive.

Quotations and the people a rule applies to

Words inside quotation marks are copied exactly from the source page. Before a new guide or a correction is published, every quotation is matched word for word against the source page, and every quotation, figure, deadline and eligibility rule is checked again for whom it applies to: Original Medicare or a Medicare Advantage plan, Medicaid, private pay or the VA; a hospital, a nursing home, assisted living or home; Illinois or another state; and the year. A sentence that is accurate for one of those cases is not presented as the rule for all of them.

Every link in a guide’s list of sources is checked to open before the guide is published.

Dates and corrections

Each guide shows the date its figures were read from their sources and the date it was last reviewed. Programmes change their figures, so the date tells you how current the page is.

Guides already on the site are re-checked against their sources one at a time. When a check finds a sentence that no longer matches its source, the sentence is corrected and the guide’s review date changes.

What the guides do not do

The guides do not rank or recommend senior living communities. They are educational and are not medical, legal or financial advice: confirm anything that matters with the agency, plan or provider directly.
