Privacy

What this site collects, and what happens to it

Plain version first: your six answers are never sent anywhere. The only place that asks you for anything is the optional tour-inquiry form — shown after your result, or reached directly from a guide. It asks for no health information, and its purpose is to pass your name, contact details, the type of care you are interested in and, if you took the six questions, the care level those questions suggested to Westminster Place Admissions so they can contact you about a tour. The site also keeps a simple tally of how often its steps are used, with no names and no answers in it — explained below under “How visits are counted”.

The assessment collects nothing

The six questions run entirely in your browser. Your answers are never sent anywhere, never stored, and never attached to your name. Close the tab and they are gone. The server refuses a submission outright if any answer field is present, so this is enforced rather than promised. What does travel, if you use the form, is the care level those answers produced — one label out of six, such as “Assisted Living”. The reasoning behind it does not.

One thing is tallied, and it is worth being exact about. When a result appears, your browser adds one mark to the site’s count for that care category. The request carries no answers and no name. The details are under “How visits are counted”.

What the tour-inquiry form collects

If you ask to be contacted about a tour, this is the complete list:

  • First name and last name
  • Phone number, email address, or both — whichever you give
  • The type of care you said you were interested in — one of the fixed choices on the form
  • Whether you took the six questions, and if you did, the care level they produced — one of six fixed labels, never your individual answers. If you came straight to the form from a guide, the record says the assessment was not taken, and nothing about your care needs is held.
  • The date and time you submitted, generated by the server
  • A unique reference number, generated by the server
  • The exact permission wording you were shown when you ticked the box, its version, and the time you agreed, stored word for word alongside your record
  • Bookkeeping the site adds itself: the name of this site as the source, the Westminster Place team and phone number that match the care level (their general inquiries line if you did not take the questions), and whether your inquiry has been passed on yet (it starts as “not yet” and is only ever changed by hand)

That is everything. No individual assessment answers. No diagnosis, medications, medical history or date of birth — none of which were ever asked for. The server rejects a submission outright if any field of that kind is present, rather than accepting it and discarding it later. The care level is the single value derived from your answers that is kept, and it is kept so the right Admissions team is the one that calls you.

Why it is collected

For two purposes, and no others. First, so your details can be passed to Westminster Place Admissions and they can contact you about a tour. Second, so there is a dated record that your inquiry came through this guide.

It does not schedule anything and it does not guarantee a tour — Admissions decide that. It does not put you on a mailing list; there is no mailing list. Nobody is paid for your inquiry.

Who operates this site

This guide was independently developed by an employee of Westminster Place. It is not an official Westminster Place website. No referral fees are received from Westminster Place or anyone else.

Where it is stored, and who else touches it

Three outside companies are involved, and no others:

  • Netlify hosts the site and stores the submission records. Records are held in a private store that is not reachable from the public internet.
  • Resend delivers the notification email that tells the site operator a submission arrived. That email contains your name, contact details and care level, so Resend processes and retains those under their own terms, which this site does not control.
  • Every request you make — opening a page, submitting the form, or one of the step counts described below — reaches Netlify's servers, which keep the standard log that any web host keeps: the address your connection came from (an IP address), the kind of browser and device you used, which page or file was requested, the time, and whether it succeeded. Netlify holds that log under its own terms, and this site does not control how long. For the form, that log shows the submission reference and whether the save succeeded; it does not contain your name, email or phone number.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface. That is the only outside request the page itself makes, and it means Google's servers see the same kind of technical details, such as your IP address and browser type, when the font is fetched.

There is no analytics service, no advertising network and no cookie, and the page loads no other outside service.

How visits are counted

This site keeps a simple tally of how often its steps are used — how many times the questions were started, a result was shown, the form was opened — so the person who runs it can see what helps. Each mark in the tally is one small request from your browser for an empty file named after the step. The request carries no answers, no name and no visitor ID, and the site sets no cookie. It is a count of actions, not of people: one person can add several marks, and a browser that blocks the request adds none.

Like every request, these land in Netlify’s request log, which records the IP address and browser details described above. This site adds nothing to that log and has no way of its own to recognise a returning visitor; what Netlify could work out from its log is governed by Netlify’s terms, not by this notice.

What exactly is counted?
  • Arriving on the home page, with a one-word note of how you arrived — “direct”, or the name of a site that linked here — and never the full address of the page you came from
  • Whether you are answering for yourself or for someone else
  • Starting the six questions, passing the second and the fourth, and starting again
  • Seeing a result, named for the care category it was — for example “result-assisted” — never the answers behind it
  • Opening the costs section or the tour questions
  • Seeing the Westminster Place card, and clicking through to Westminster Place’s website or to the state’s facility directory
  • Opening the tour-inquiry form, submitting it, and the record being saved
  • Starting and finishing the payment navigator
  • Arriving from one of the guides, and which of the guide’s choices you took — the questions, the form, or the payment navigator

The same one-word note of how you arrived is also attached when you finish the questions, submit the form, or click through to Westminster Place. That is the whole list. The counts are used to see how often each step is used, and for nothing else.

Sharing with Westminster Place

Your information is not shared with Westminster Place unless you ask for it to be, by ticking the permission box on the form. The box is unticked by default and the form cannot be submitted without it, because sharing is the whole point of submitting it. If you do not want your details passed on, do not use this form. Westminster Place publishes its own contact details and you can reach them without this site at all — their general number is (847) 570-3422 and their website is presbyterianliving.org. Nothing is lost by going direct; this guide keeps no record of you if you do.

If you do tick it, here is exactly what happens. Your first name, last name, phone number, email address, the type of care you said you were interested in and, if you took the six questions, the care level the questions suggested are given to Westminster Place Admissions so they can contact you. If you did not take them, the type of care you chose on the form is all that is known about what you are looking for. That is a transfer of your information to them, and this notice is not telling you otherwise. It is done by hand, by a person, over the phone — this website sends nothing to Westminster Place automatically and has no connection to their systems.

What is never shared: your individual assessment answers, which never left your device, and any health information, because none was ever collected. Nothing is shared with anyone other than Westminster Place Admissions.

You can withdraw this permission at any time using the address below. If your details have already been passed to Admissions, withdrawing stops anything further here, but it cannot undo that call — you would need to ask Westminster Place directly to remove what they hold.

How long it is kept

The submission record is kept for 180 days from the date you submit it, then deleted automatically. This is enforced by the system itself rather than by someone remembering: expired records are removed whenever the form runs, and each run writes a dated receipt so the policy can be checked rather than taken on trust.

One honest limit, and it is a real one. The 180-day rule governs the record held by this site. When you submit, a notification email is also sent to the operator, and that email contains your name, phone number, email address and care level (or a note that the assessment was not taken), and the type of care you said you were interested in, so he can make the call without a lookup. That message sits in his mailbox and with the email provider, Resend — neither of which the automatic 180-day deletion can reach. He clears it manually once the referral is made. If you ask to be removed, say so and the mailbox copy is deleted too.

A second honest limit. Once your details have been given to Westminster Place Admissions, what they do with them is governed by their own privacy practices, not by this notice. The 180-day rule deletes this site's copy. It has no power over theirs.

Asking to be removed

Write to norvenph@gmail.com and your record will be deleted. You do not need to give a reason, and you do not need your reference number — your name is enough. You can also ask what is held about you, and you will be sent it. Either request is answered within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

That address is read by the person who operates this guide. If your details have already been passed to Westminster Place Admissions, deleting the record here stops anything further from this site, but it cannot withdraw what they already hold — ask Westminster Place directly for that.
